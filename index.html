Dryer Vent Cleaning

A clogged dryer vent is one of the most common and preventable causes of house fires. Lint buildup restricts airflow, forces your dryer to work harder and creates dangerous heat conditions. Our dryer vent cleaning service clears the entire exhaust path from the appliance to the exterior vent cap. You will notice your clothes drying faster, your energy bills dropping and your dryer lasting longer. Chimney Cleaning

Regular chimney cleaning is essential for safe fireplace and wood-burning stove operation. Creosote, a byproduct of wood combustion, builds up inside chimney flues and is highly flammable. Our professional chimney sweep service removes creosote deposits, clears bird nests and debris, and inspects the flue for cracks or damage. Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our air duct and dryer vent cleaning services in Norwalk, CT.

How often should I have my air ducts cleaned?

The EPA and NADCA recommend having your air ducts professionally cleaned every 3 to 5 years. However, if you have pets, allergies, recent renovation work, visible mold or a new home purchase, more frequent cleaning may be beneficial. Our team will assess your specific situation and recommend the right schedule.

How often should dryer vents be cleaned?

Dryer vents should be cleaned at least once per year for average household use. If you do laundry frequently or notice your clothes taking longer to dry, a burning smell or excessive heat from the dryer, you should schedule cleaning immediately. Regular cleaning significantly reduces fire risk.

Do you serve residential and commercial properties?

Yes, we serve both residential homes and commercial properties throughout Norwalk, CT and the surrounding area. Our commercial cleaning is available for offices, restaurants, retail spaces, medical facilities and industrial buildings. We offer flexible scheduling to work around your business hours.

Are your technicians certified?

Absolutely. All Amazon Air Duct technicians undergo a stringent certification process before working independently on customer properties. Our team includes both onsite technicians and training managers who bring years of combined experience in indoor air quality and duct cleaning to every job.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

A typical residential air duct cleaning takes between 2 to 4 hours depending on the size of your home and the condition of the ductwork. Commercial jobs vary based on the size and complexity of the system. We give you an accurate time estimate before starting so you can plan accordingly.
